中国科学院上海有机化学研究所（简称：上海有机所）是集基础研究、应用研究和高技术创新研究为一体的综合性化学研究机构，创建于1950年5月，是中国科学院首批成立的15个研究所之一。

博士后流动站
更多 +
中国科学院上海有机化学研究所于1985年7月被批准建立博士后流动站，现有金属有机化学国家重点实验室、生命过程小分子调控全国重点实验室、先进氟氮材料重点实验室（中国科学院）。此外，还有中国科学院生物与化学交叉研究中心、沪港化学合成联合实验室、宁波新材料创制中心等多个联合研究中心。设站至今，已有近700名有志于有机化学工作的青年科研人员进入中国科学院上海有机化学研究所化学流动站，先后获得中国博士后基金委、中国科学院、上海市的各项科研资助和奖励200余项。
上海有机所博士后工作由人力资源处负责，包括办理博士后进出站手续、户口申请、博士后科学基金及各项资助申报、博士后经费管理、博士后联谊会联络等，并负责联系上级主管部门和兄弟单位。

The SIOC–ACS Medicinal Chemistry Forum (SIOC–ACS 2026), jointly organized by the Shanghai Institute of Organic Chemistry (SIOC) and the American Chemical Society (ACS), will be held in Shanghai from November 8 to 10, 2026. The theme of this conference is “Chemistry × Biology: Cross-Disciplinary Innovation Accelerate Medical Transformation.” Focusing on interdisciplinary collaboration as a driving force for innovation in drug development, the forum will bring together experts and scholars from China and abroad in medicinal chemistry, organic chemistry, chemical biology, and related fields to exchange cutting-edge research advances, explore new theories, technologies, and strategies arising from interdisciplinary integration, and promote connections between fundamental research, drug discovery, and translational development.
The forum is expected to attract approximately 300 participants. Several internationally renowned scientists will attend the conference, including Professor Craig W. Lindsley of Vanderbilt University, USA, Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Medicinal Chemistry; Professor Bradley S. Moore of the University of California, San Diego, USA, Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Natural Products; and Professor Jayanta Haldar of the Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research, India, Editor-in-Chief of ACS Infectious Diseases. More than 40 scholars will deliver oral presentations. A poster session will also be held. The forum will organize a Young Scientists Poster Forum to encourage young researchers from academia and industry to participate, with awards presented for outstanding posters. For more information about the conference, please visit the ACS Publications WeChat Official Account:
https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/9-4c3D2TZluCdwv-MhvVZA
We welcome registrations for this conference. As venue capacity is limited, please register as soon as possible. Those whose registrations are accepted will receive an email confirming their participation.
Scientific instrument and reagent manufacturers are warmly invited to exhibit and sponsor the forum.
